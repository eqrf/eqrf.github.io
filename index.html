📖 Pocket Glossary (Grade 7 & 8 Science Translator)
1. Bronchiole Tiny air tubes in your lungs as thin as spaghetti.
2. Epithelial Tissue Protective sheet of tightly packed cells lining your airways.
3. Cilia Microscopic sweeping brooms on top of airway cells.
4. Syncytium Giant multi-nucleated cell blob formed when RSV melts neighboring cell membranes together.
5. H&E Stain Hematoxylin purple DNA dye & Eosin pink cytoplasm dye.
6. Antibodies Y-shaped proteins that block RSV from attaching to cells.
7. Virus vs. Bacteria Viruses hide inside human cells and cannot be killed by antibiotics.
