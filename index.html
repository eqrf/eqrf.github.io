📖 Detective's Pocket Glossary (Science Terms → Everyday Words)
Bronchioles Tiny air tubes inside your lungs—as thin as a strand of spaghetti!
Cilia Microscopic sweeping brooms on top of airway cells that brush mucus and germs out.
Basement Membrane The cell foundation floor that holds a sheet of airway cells together.
Syncytium (sin-SISH-ee-um) A giant fused multi-nucleus cell created when RSV melts neighbor cell walls together.
Hematoxylin & Eosin (H&E) Two microscope dyes: Purple DNA dye (nuclei) & Pink fluid dye (cytoplasm).
Fusion (F) Protein RSV's surface "melting key" that causes neighboring cell membranes to fuse into one blob.
